About PitchProof

The player behind
the evidence.

Football is bigger than a single clip or a number. PitchProof brings the record together without pretending it tells the whole story.

Our starting point

Give the work context.
Give the player control.

A player should be able to record their matches, keep track of development and decide what to share. A scout should be able to understand where information comes from and ask a thoughtful follow-up.

That is the role of the Football Passport: a connected record, not a promise of discovery or a shortcut to a contract.

01 / Evidence

Be clear about the source.

Self-reported information and AI observations need context. Missing evidence should never become an invented certainty.

02 / Agency

Let the player choose.

Public sharing and scout discovery are deliberate choices, with additional safeguards for young players.

03 / Responsibility

Keep judgement human.

Comparison does not need a winner. A useful scouting tool supports considered decisions, not speculative potential rankings.
