Cookie and Tracking Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

In short
  • ScoutX has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no cross-app tracking.
  • We only store what is technically necessary, such as keeping you signed in.

In the app

The app stores data on your device only to work: your sign-in session, language and appearance preferences, offline copies of your own data, and in-progress uploads so they can resume. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

On the website and guardian pages

The pitchproof.online website and guardian approval pages use browser storage only where needed for sign-in and security. The guardian page removes the approval link from the address bar after loading and does not set tracking cookies.

Security signals

Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. It does not track your activity.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, a diagnostic report is sent to our crash reporting provider with personal data removed. It is used only to fix bugs.

The same text is available in the app under Settings → Privacy Center.
