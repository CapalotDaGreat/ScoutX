2Eligibility

Players of any age may use PitchProof. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must approve your account before you can use it.

If you are under 13, your account is private: nobody else can see your profile, find you or contact you, you cannot share videos or receive trial invitations, and you can only use the player role. A parent or legal guardian must give verifiable consent as described in the Youth Safety Policy. Without that consent the account is deleted after 14 days, and if the parent withdraws consent the account is deleted.

Scouts and coaches must be 18 or older and must act on behalf of a genuine club, academy or organisation. Give your real age group; we may restrict or close accounts where we believe it is wrong.
