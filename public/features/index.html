The product

Evidence, connected.

From your first match log to a scout follow-up. A closer look at what PitchProof does, and where its limits are.

01 / Football Passport

Your game in context.

Your player profile, match record and chosen highlights in one shareable place. Choose public sections and keep the origin of information clear.

  • Profile and football background
  • Logged match and season information
  • Selected highlights and endorsements
  • Sharing and visibility controls
The player journey ↗

02 / Performance

A record you can build on.

Log matches and statistics, track basic development goals and write a post-match reflection in your own words. Match logging and basic development stay free.

Analysis trends require at least three analyses. A reflection is personal and is excluded from the public Passport.

03 / Media & observations

Footage is a starting point.

Upload highlights and use Reel Studio within usage limits. Video analysis can provide still-frame AI observations to review and correct.

Continuous player tracking, automated events and pitch maps are unavailable. AI observations are not verified performance measurements.

04 / Scout workspace

Research. Organise. Follow up.

Discovery & comparison

Search opted-in players and compare evidence side by side. No overall or potential-grade sorting, and no declared winner.

Pipeline

Save players, filter by stage and search, and keep private workspace notes.

Requests & trials

Ask for more evidence and manage trial invitations. Guardian co-approval applies for minors.
