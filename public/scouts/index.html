For scouts

A clearer view.
A considered next step.

Discover players who choose to be found. Look at the evidence in context, organise your thinking and follow up responsibly.

01 / Discovery

Find a player.
Understand the record.

Search opted-in players and refine your discovery. Review their Passport before deciding what evidence you need next.

Side-by-side comparison is neutral: no winner, no overall or potential-grade sorting. Your judgement remains yours.

02 / Pipeline

Keep the next step clear.

Save players to your pipeline, filter by stage, search your workspace and keep private notes alongside your research.

  1. SaveKeep a player in your working pipeline.
  2. ContextualiseRecord private notes and review available evidence.
  3. Follow upRequest missing evidence or prepare a trial invitation.

03 / Requests & trials

A proper invitation.
A clear response.

Request evidence and send trial invitations with details a player can review. Track the response instead of losing the next step.

Minor-player requests require guardian co-approval. An invitation is not a contract or a guarantee of selection.
