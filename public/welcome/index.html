How it works for players

From matchday to your next step.

Three habits, one app. Everything starts with what you actually did.

  1. Log the match

    Result, minutes, goals and assists you know. Then a short reflection in your own words while it is fresh. Reflections stay private.

  2. Work on one thing

    Set a development goal, link training to it and see what your match log shows. No invented potential scores.

  3. Share on your terms

    Turn on your public Passport and choose its sections. Scout discovery is a separate opt-in. Self-reported details are labelled.

For scouts

Discovery with context, not a crystal ball.

Every card shows where the information comes from and how much of it there is. When the evidence is thin, PitchProof says so instead of filling the gap with a rating.

  • Only players who opted in to discovery
  • Matches logged, analysed samples and freshness on every card
  • No overall or potential grades, and comparisons never declare a winner
  • Contact starts with a trial invitation or evidence request; guardians co-approve for minors

Before you start

Good questions.

Can I download PitchProof now?

Not yet. PitchProof is in development and is not available on the App Store. This website has no sign-up or download.

What does it cost?

Match logging, basic development goals, safety and privacy tools are always free. An optional Premium plan is planned to raise usage limits; nothing can be bought yet. See the Subscription Terms.

Who can see my Passport?

Only what you publish. You choose the public sections, and appearing in scout discovery is a separate opt-in. Under-13 accounts remain private.

Does PitchProof rate or rank players?

No. There are no overall or potential grades, comparisons do not declare a winner, and PitchProof does not guarantee trials, contracts or selection.
