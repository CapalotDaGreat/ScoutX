Football Passport

One link. Your whole game.

Claim your own pitchproof.online link and share it with coaches, clubs and scouts. Your Passport brings your profile, season stats, career and endorsements together on one page.

  • Self-reported details are clearly labelled as self-reported
  • Choose exactly which sections are public
  • Export a football CV as a PDF

Match log

Every match counts.

Log minutes, goals, assists, passing, tackles and your rating after each game. PitchProof builds your season stats and trends from what you record.

  • Nothing is estimated: anything you didn't record stays empty
  • Season totals, averages and recent form
  • Match logging is always free

Development

Goals that track themselves.

Set a target, like more shots on goal from midfield. PitchProof measures your progress against your logged matches and suggests what to do next.

  • Progress measured from your own match data
  • Log training sessions and link them to goals
  • Matchday prep and a weekly football report

Endorsements & milestones

Let your coach vouch for you.

Send your coach a link to write an endorsement for your Passport. Milestones mark your journey as your Passport grows, from claiming your link to your first coach endorsement.

  • Endorsements show who wrote them and their role
  • Hide any endorsement you don't want to show
  • Build a career timeline of clubs and highlights

Scout discovery

Get discovered, on your terms.

Scout discovery is off until you switch it on. When you opt in, scouts using PitchProof can find your Passport, save you to their shortlist and send you a trial invitation.

  • You decide if and when scouts can find you
  • Trial invitations arrive with full details
  • For players under 18, scout contact needs a guardian's permission
