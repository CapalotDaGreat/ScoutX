A few things worth knowing about access, evidence and control.

Can I download PitchProof now?

Not yet. PitchProof is in development and is not available on the App Store. There is no active store download or sign-up promise on this website.

What is a Football Passport?

A shareable player record bringing together profile information, logged matches and selected evidence such as highlights. Players choose which sections are public.

Explore the player journey.

Can every scout find my profile?

Scout discovery is opt-in, not automatic. Visibility and youth-safety rules apply. Under-13 accounts remain private.

How are young players protected?

Youth features require guardian consent, with guardian co-approval for minor-player evidence requests and trials. Under-13 accounts remain private and require the applicable parental consent process.

Read the Guardian Consent Notice and Youth Safety Policy for the full rules.

Does video analysis track every player?

No. The implemented analysis uses still frames and AI observations. Continuous tracking, automated events and pitch maps are unavailable. Short-clip device processing and real-device behaviour still need validation.

Review observations critically. Corrections are feedback, not model training. Analysis trends require at least three analyses.

Does PitchProof rank player potential?

No overall or potential-grade sorting is offered in scout discovery. Side-by-side comparison is neutral and does not declare a winner. PitchProof does not guarantee trials, contracts or selection.

What is free, and what is Premium?

Match logging, basic development, safety and privacy tools are always free. Optional Premium is intended to raise usage limits. Purchasing and restore require store and device validation; no subscription can be purchased here.

Read the Subscription Terms.

Are these native-device screenshots?

No. The product previews are Expo web previews with demonstration data, not native-device captures or customer accounts. The campaign football image is AI-generated and illustrative.

Is my post-match reflection public?

No. Your own-word reflection is excluded from the public Passport projection.

How do I export data or delete my account?

Use account settings in the app. If you cannot access your account, follow the account deletion instructions or contact support. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Where can I report a concern?

Use the reporting page for a profile, media or safety concern. Reporting and blocking are never paywalled. For other help, contact PitchProof.
