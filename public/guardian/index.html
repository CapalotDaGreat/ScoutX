A private account

  • Nobody else can see your child's profile, find it in search or contact them. There is no public Passport, no scout contact, no trial invitations, no endorsements and no sharing of videos.
  • There is no advertising or automatic subscription promotion. Optional purchases, where offered, go through Apple, where Ask to Buy and Screen Time let you control them.

What we collect and why

  • So far: your email address, your child's first name and the sign-in details of the account, only to ask for your permission. Without permission within 14 days of sign-up, all of it is deleted.
  • With your permission: the name and profile details your child enters, their age group (never a date of birth), matches, training, goals, videos they upload, movement summaries measured on the phone (no location), push notification tokens and security logs.
  • We use this only to run the app for your child. Hosting, storage and email delivery are handled by service providers acting on our instructions, listed in the Privacy Policy. AI analysis is only used if you tick it below.
  • We never use your child's data to train AI models and never disclose it to third parties for their own purposes.
