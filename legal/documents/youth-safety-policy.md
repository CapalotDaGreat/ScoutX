## Age

When you create an account you choose an age band: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older. We do not ask for your date of birth. You can move to a stricter age band yourself; moving to an older band needs support.

## Children under 13: private mode

Accounts for children under 13 always run in private mode:

- Nobody else can see the child's profile, matches, videos or movement data. There is no public Passport and no username link.
- The child cannot be found in scout search and cannot receive scout requests, trial invitations, endorsements, coach observations or messages from anyone.
- The child cannot share videos, not even with a single person.
- The child can only use the player role. Scout and coach roles are not available.
- The child can log matches and training, set development goals, keep videos in a private library, record movement sessions (no location is stored) and, if the parent allows it, get AI analysis of their own clips.

These limits are enforced on our servers and in our database access rules, not only in the app. A parent cannot switch them off, because the permissions that would make an account public do not exist for children under 13.

## Parental permission for children under 13

Before a child under 13 can use PitchProof, a parent or legal guardian must give permission:

1. The child enters a parent's email address and their own first name. Until permission is given, we use these and the account's sign-in details only to ask for it.
2. The parent receives a notice that explains what we collect, how we use it and their rights, and a secure link to a consent form. The parent confirms they are the child's parent or legal guardian and 18 or older, confirms they have read the notice, chooses whether to allow AI analysis, and signs by typing their full name.
3. We then send a second email to the same address. Permission takes effect only when the parent confirms it from that email.

If permission is not given within 14 days of choosing the under-13 age band, or the parent declines, the account and its data are deleted. Permission lasts up to 12 months and then has to be renewed; if it is not renewed within 30 days of expiry, the account is deleted. A parent can withdraw permission at any time from the link in the confirmation email or by writing to {{supportEmail}}; withdrawal deletes the account.

## Guardian approval for players aged 13 to 17

Players aged 13 to 17 enter a parent's or guardian's email address. The guardian receives a secure link, valid for 7 days, that works only once and requires no account. The guardian confirms they are the parent or legal guardian and chooses each permission:

- public Passport;
- discovery by scouts;
- contact from scouts through PitchProof requests;
- public media;
- public display of movement data;
- AI analysis.

Nothing is allowed by default. Approval lasts up to 12 months and can be withdrawn at any time.

## What stays private for minors

Until a parent or guardian approves, the player sees a waiting screen where they can resend the request, sign out or delete the account. After approval, private features such as logging matches and goals and recording movement sessions work regardless of which permissions were granted, because they stay private and movement sessions store no location. For players aged 13 to 17, anything the guardian has not allowed stays off: the Passport stays private, they cannot be found in scout search and scouts cannot send them requests or trials.

## Public display

Players aged 13 to 17 appear publicly only where their guardian allowed it, with first name and last initial only, labelled "Youth player". Exact age, school, contact details and location are never shown. Children under 13 never appear publicly. Lock-screen notifications for all minors use generic text.

## Scouts and trials

Scouts can contact a player aged 13 to 17 only through PitchProof's request system, never by private message, and the guardian receives an email when a scout makes a request. A minor can accept a trial only after the guardian approves it through a separate secure link. Scouts cannot contact children under 13 at all.

## Advertising and promotions

PitchProof shows no advertising. Minors do not see subscription promotions outside Settings, and the paywall is never shown to them automatically. Any purchase goes through Apple, where parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

## Reporting

Anyone can report concerns about the safety of a young player. These reports are reviewed first.
