## Ownership

You keep ownership of your content. PitchProof's own software, design, name and logo belong to {{entityName}}.

## Third-party content

Do not upload broadcasts, club footage, music or other material unless you own it or have permission. Club crests and competition marks remain the property of their owners.

## Takedown notices

If you believe content on PitchProof infringes your rights, send a notice to {{legalEmail}} with:

- your name and contact details;
- the work you believe is infringed;
- where the content is on PitchProof (a link or the player's Passport name);
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised;
- a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

You can also use "Report" with the reason "Copyright". We review notices, may restrict the content while we review it, and notify the uploader.

## Counter-notices

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can appeal in the app or write to {{legalEmail}} explaining why you have the right to use it.

## Repeat infringement

Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing content may be suspended or disabled.
