## In the app

The app stores data on your device only to work: your sign-in session, language and appearance preferences, offline copies of your own data, and in-progress uploads so they can resume. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## On the website and guardian pages

The pitchproof.online website and guardian approval pages use browser storage only where needed for sign-in and security. The guardian page removes the approval link from the address bar after loading and does not set tracking cookies.

The public policy pages do not require a PitchProof account and do not embed advertising or audience-analytics trackers. Hosting and security providers still receive network request metadata, including an IP address and user agent. A guardian approval or withdrawal link contains a sensitive token: do not forward it or publish it. Clearing your browser history is not a withdrawal of consent.

Links to Apple, external football websites and other services open destinations with their own storage and privacy rules. If we add optional tracking in future, we must update this notice and obtain any legally required consent before enabling it; the present policy is not advance consent.

## Security signals

Firebase App Check supplies device/app-attestation signals used to assess whether a request is legitimate. This is a security control, not advertising or cross-app tracking, and it is not an absolute guarantee that every request is genuine.

## Crash reports

Where configured and enabled, Sentry receives crash and sampled performance diagnostics to help fix faults and reliability issues. Default personal-data collection is disabled and redaction is applied; this is not a guarantee that every diagnostic is anonymous. It is not an advertising or audience-analytics service. Provider retention and region must be confirmed before launch, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Managing device and browser storage

You can change system permissions, clear browser storage or uninstall the app using device controls. Doing so may sign you out or remove local drafts and offline data; it does not delete your server account, withdraw a guardian approval or cancel an Apple subscription. Use the in-app account controls or contact {{supportEmail}} for those actions.
