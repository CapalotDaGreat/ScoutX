## Your responsibility

Before uploading, make sure that you recorded the footage yourself or have permission from the person or club who did, and that recording was allowed at the venue and competition. You also need any consent required from people who can be identified, in particular minors and their guardians.

## Visibility

Every upload starts as private. You can add a video to your Passport, or share it with one specific scout for a limited time. Team-only and unlisted-link sharing are not available. Before media becomes visible beyond you, PitchProof shows a warning and a preview of who will be able to see it, and asks you to confirm that you have the right to share it. Players aged 13 to 17 can add media to their Passport only if their guardian has allowed it. Media of children under 13 always stays private and cannot be shared.

The first time you upload, PitchProof asks you to acknowledge this policy. PitchProof records that acknowledgement with the policy version.

## Processing

Uploaded files first go to a protected quarantine area. Our servers check the file type, container, codecs, duration, resolution and size before the file is moved to your private storage. Files that fail these checks are rejected and deleted. These checks are not a malware or virus scan, and PitchProof does not claim to perform one.

## Analysis

Before any analysis, PitchProof asks for your consent for that video and records it with the policy version. You can withdraw it while the analysis runs; processing then stops and temporary files are deleted.

- Short clips (up to 60 seconds): still frames are taken on your phone and only those frames are uploaded. The video itself stays on your phone.
- Longer videos: the video is uploaded, still frames are extracted on our servers and reviewed part by part.

Frames are sent to an AI provider under the conditions in the AI Use Policy. PitchProof does not use facial recognition. You confirm which frames show you; coaching about "you" is only shown after you confirm. Computer-vision tracking of individual players is not available at this time and is marked as such in your report.

## What is kept after an analysis

- The original video is deleted after the analysis unless you choose "Keep match video". If you keep it, it is filed privately in your media library.
- Frames and other intermediate files that the report does not cite are deleted.
- Only the frames the report cites as evidence are kept, privately, until you delete the analysis or your account.
- Short evidence clips (up to 6 silent clips of about 8 seconds around cited moments) are made only if you ask for them.
- If an analysis fails, the video is kept for 24 hours so you can retry, or for 7 days if you chose that option, and then deleted.

## People who appear in videos

If you appear in a video on PitchProof, or a minor in your care does, you can report it with the reason "I appear in this video" or "A minor appears in this video", including without a PitchProof account. While a report is under review the video may be hidden from public view.

## Removal

You can delete your media at any time. Deleted media is removed from storage, including derived frames and highlight files, as described in the Data Retention Policy.
