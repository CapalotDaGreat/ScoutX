## What Premium includes

Premium increases the monthly limits for frame analyses, full-match analyses and reel exports, and allows more analyses at the same time. Current limits are shown in the app before you buy. Premium does not improve your visibility to scouts or your chances of a trial.

The purchase screen and Apple's confirmation show the subscription name, billing period, local price and any trial. Availability and technical/privacy restrictions still apply; Premium does not enable disabled AI, unavailable tracking or unvalidated measurements. Do not purchase for a capability that is marked unavailable.

## Always free

Safety features, privacy controls, account deletion, data export, reporting, guardian controls, match logging and basic development features never require a subscription.

## Billing

Subscriptions are purchased through your Apple ID and billed by Apple. They renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm purchase. Apple may charge renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price shown under its applicable terms. Monthly and annual options, where offered, are different billing periods; usage quotas are not a promise of unlimited processing.

## Cancellation and account deletion

Use Settings, Plan & usage, Manage subscription in PitchProof, or https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal, not access for the remainder of a paid period, subject to refunds or Apple's adjustments.

Deleting PitchProof, deleting your PitchProof account, signing out or withdrawing guardian permission does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel renewal separately with Apple to avoid further charges. You can still delete your PitchProof account immediately; deletion is not conditional on waiting for a subscription to expire.

## Restore and account access

Use Restore purchases in Plan & usage with the Apple account used to purchase and the intended PitchProof account. Purchases are linked to your PitchProof account identifier through RevenueCat. If restoration or account switching assigns access incorrectly, contact {{supportEmail}}; we do not promise automatic transfer between unrelated accounts or Family Sharing unless explicitly offered.

## Free trials

If a free trial is offered, it converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel beforehand.

The exact eligibility, length, price after trial and cancellation conditions are shown before purchase. Follow Apple's trial cancellation deadline, including cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends where specified. No trial is promised unless it appears in the actual offer.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

You can request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/. Cancelling does not itself request a refund. Nothing here limits mandatory cancellation, withdrawal, refund or other consumer rights that apply to your purchase.

## Changes and limits

We may change Premium features or prices with notice. Usage limits apply per 30-day period and reset as shown in the app. If a payment fails, Apple may provide a grace period during which Premium continues.

Usage windows are anchored to the account's first metered use and can differ from Apple's monthly or annual billing dates. Unused capacity is not a cash balance. A price increase is subject to Apple's applicable notice/consent process and mandatory law. If access does not appear after a successful purchase, restore purchases or contact support rather than buying again.

## Minors

Players under 18 do not see subscription promotions outside Settings, and the paywall is never shown to them automatically. Children under 13 can use every private feature without a subscription. Purchases by minors go through Apple and are subject to Apple's family and parental controls, such as Ask to Buy.
