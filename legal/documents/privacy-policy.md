## What we collect

- Account data: name, email address, sign-in provider (email, Apple or Google), your role (player, scout or coach) and an age band (under 13, 13 to 17, 18 or older). We never ask for or store your date of birth.
- Profile data you enter: club, positions, strong foot, nationality, biography, career history and achievements.
- Football data you enter: match logs, statistics, development goals, notes and coach observations.
- Links you add to supported football websites: the link, which website it belongs to, the type of page, the matches you link it to and who you share it with. Links are private until you share them. We do not open, read or copy anything from those websites, and a link does not make any information verified.
- Media you upload: videos, photos and the metadata needed to process them (duration, resolution, file size, codec).
- Analysis results: AI-generated observations, coaching suggestions and highlight plans created from your media.
- Movement sessions: distance, speed, sprints, zones and a heatmap expressed in metres relative to the pitch. See "Movement sessions" below.
- Scouting data: if you are a scout or coach, the players you save, your reports and your trial invitations. If you are a player, reports and invitations that scouts address to you.
- Guardian data (players under 18): your parent's or guardian's email address and their consent decisions. For children under 13, also the name the parent typed to sign the consent form.
- Device and technical data: push notification token, app version, crash diagnostics and security signals (Firebase App Check).
- Subscription data: the Firebase account identifier used by RevenueCat, product, purchase, renewal and entitlement events. Apple handles payment details; PitchProof does not receive your full payment-card details.
- Website, support and safety data: network request metadata such as IP address and user agent processed by hosting/security providers, messages you send us, reports, appeals and records of consent and deletion requests.

We receive most data from you. Other users can provide observations, reports, invitations or footage in which you appear. Apple, RevenueCat and our infrastructure providers supply transaction and technical information. Photos and videos can identify teammates, opponents, spectators or their voices; private visibility does not remove the need to have permission to upload and process them. Do not include medical records or other unnecessary sensitive information in notes or footage.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

## Permissions and choices

Photos access is used for media you choose to upload. Location is requested only for recording movement, and notification permission is optional. You can change permissions in your device settings. Refusing a permission prevents the feature that needs it, not unrelated match logging or privacy controls. Recording is foreground-only; we do not run background location tracking.

## Movement sessions

While you actively record a session, with the app open and "While Using" location permission, your phone reads its position to measure distance and draw a heatmap. Each reading is converted on your device into metres relative to where the session started. The latitude and longitude are discarded in memory and are never written to your device storage, our servers, crash reports or logs. We store only the pitch-relative summary. Because no location is stored, this feature is available to every user, including players under 18.

## Why we use your data (legal bases)

- To provide PitchProof as you request it (contract): your account, profile, Passport, match logs, media library, analysis and scouting tools.
- With your consent, or your guardian's consent for players aged 13 to 17: a public Passport, discovery by scouts, scout contact, public media and AI analysis. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- For children under 13: with the verified consent of a parent or legal guardian, for the whole private account and, separately, for AI analysis. Before that consent, only to ask for it.
- For our legitimate interests: keeping PitchProof secure, preventing abuse, fixing crashes and moderating reported content.
- To meet legal obligations: keeping proof of consent and moderation decisions for as long as the law requires.

The GDPR legal bases above apply where the GDPR governs the processing; Swiss processing is also subject to the FADP. Reading or acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent to every optional use. We use the data required to provide the feature you request; optional public sharing and AI choices are separate. If you do not supply necessary account information, we cannot provide an account. We do not use your data for targeted advertising or sell it.

## Who receives your data

- Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, file storage, server functions, hosting).
- OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to, for analysis you request. Requests are sent with data collection denied and zero data retention required.
- Sentry, if diagnostics are enabled, for crash and performance diagnostics. We disable default personal-data collection and apply redaction controls, but cannot promise that every free-text diagnostic is anonymous.
- Apple and Expo, to deliver push notifications.
- RevenueCat and Apple, if you buy a subscription.
- An email delivery provider, to send guardian approval emails.
- Other PitchProof users, only as far as your visibility settings allow. Nothing about a child under 13 is ever shown to other users.

A public Passport or public media can be viewed by people outside your country and may be copied or indexed outside PitchProof. Changing visibility stops further access through PitchProof, but cannot recall screenshots or copies someone already made. A link to another football website takes you to a service with its own privacy terms; we store the link, not a verified copy of that site's content.

We may disclose data when necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request or protect people from harm. We limit disclosure to what is necessary.

## Youth players

Everyone under 18 needs a parent's or guardian's approval before PitchProof becomes active. Players aged 13 to 17: the guardian chooses each permission separately and can withdraw it at any time. Where public features are allowed, minors are shown with first name and last initial only, labelled as a youth player, and never with an exact age. See the Youth Safety Policy and the Guardian Consent Notice.

## Children under {{privateModeUntilAge}}

Accounts for children under {{privateModeUntilAge}} are private. Nobody else can see the child's profile, find it or contact the child, and the child cannot share videos, receive trial invitations or use any role other than player. There is no advertising and no data is sold.

Before a child under {{privateModeUntilAge}} can use PitchProof, we ask a parent or legal guardian for verifiable consent. Until then we hold only the parent's email address, the child's first name and the account's sign-in details, and we use them only to ask. The parent receives a notice describing what we collect and why, signs a consent form by typing their full name, and confirms it from a second email. If consent is not given within 14 days, we delete the account and this information. Consent lasts up to 12 months and must then be renewed.

Once consent is given, we collect only what the child enters or uploads and the technical data listed above, and we use it only to provide the private account. AI analysis is used only if the parent separately allows it. We do not disclose a child's information to third parties for their own purposes; the service providers listed above process it on our instructions.

A parent can at any time review the information we hold about their child, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and refuse further collection or use by withdrawing consent, using the link in their confirmation email or by writing to {{privacyEmail}}. Withdrawing consent deletes the account. We may ask for information to confirm that a request comes from the parent.

## How long we keep data

Most account data stays until you delete it or your account. Temporary files have shorter schedules. Account deletion is a server-side process with progress and retries, not a promise that every copy disappears instantly. Limited consent, moderation and deletion records have separate retention periods. Apple purchase history, RevenueCat customer records and externally copied material are not all removed by the in-app deletion job. See the Data Retention Policy and contact {{privacyEmail}} for help with processor-held records.

## AI and decisions about players

AI-assisted observations can be incomplete or wrong and are not a professional assessment, medical advice or a guarantee of a trial. Reports distinguish user-provided data from available evidence. We do not use facial recognition to identify you or make solely automated decisions to award contracts or select players. Scouts and clubs make their own decisions; you can report inaccurate information or raise concerns with support. See the AI Use Policy for the data sent and the feature's limits.

## Security
