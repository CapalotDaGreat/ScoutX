## Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate data;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- have your data deleted;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- request restriction of processing where the applicable legal conditions are met;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.

The scope of each right depends on the applicable law, purpose and legal basis. Portability does not cover every internal record or another person's information. We explain a lawful limitation or refusal rather than treating all rights as unconditional.

## How to use them

- Correct: edit your profile, matches and settings directly in the app.
- Download: Privacy Center, then "Download my data". You receive an archive with a link valid for 7 days. For security you may need to sign in again first.
- Delete: Privacy Center, then "Delete account". Deletion progress is shown in the app.
- Withdraw consent: Privacy Center, or for guardians the withdrawal link in the confirmation email.
- Anything else: contact {{privacyEmail}}. We respond within the period required by law.

You do not need an account to contact us about footage in which you appear, a child's data or a privacy concern. Tell us the account or content involved and the action requested; do not email passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary identity documents. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or authority to act for someone else.

## Response times and cost

We normally respond free of charge. Under Swiss rules the ordinary access-response period is 30 days; where the GDPR applies it is one calendar month. If the law permits an extension, we explain the reason and the new deadline within the original period. We explain any permitted fee or refusal for a manifestly unfounded or excessive request and how you can challenge it.

## Deletion and separate services

Deletion removes the account through a server-side process with progress and retry controls. Some limited records have separate retention periods; see the Data Retention Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel Apple billing or erase Apple's purchase history. RevenueCat customer records require a separate processor-side action; contact us for assistance. We cannot delete another website's original page or copies someone made outside PitchProof.

Optional AI consent can be withdrawn in the Privacy Center. Acknowledging the Privacy Policy is not the legal basis for all processing; object to a particular use or request restriction by contacting us. If you no longer want the account, use account deletion rather than assuming that withdrawing one optional permission deletes it.

## Guardians

A parent or guardian of a player aged 13 to 17 can withdraw any permission at any time and can ask us about the player's data.

## Complaints

You can complain to the {{supervisoryAuthority}} or to the data protection authority where you live.
