## B6. Six-second clip feedback (examples)

When there is too little footage for a whole-match note, the app instead comments on one short clip
(at least 6 seconds of continuous confirmed footage), and only about that clip. Two templates exist:

> **Tight:** "In this {seconds}-second clip where you are confirmed, an opponent was within 1 body height of
> you {pct}% of the time." Why (defender): "As a defender, being this close may mean you were marking or
> pressing an opponent - or that you were being pressed on the ball." Why (midfielder): "A marker this
> close limits your time on the ball. A step away before the pass can create a passing angle."
> Do: "Watch the clip: did you try to move away from your marker before the ball arrived? This says nothing
> about the rest of your match."
>
> **Free:** "In this {seconds}-second clip where you are confirmed, no opponent was within 3 body heights of
> you {pct}% of the time." Why (midfielder): "Space in midfield can make you available to receive and turn.
> Check whether there was a passing line to you." Do: "Watch the clip: did you show for the ball while you
> were free? This says nothing about the rest of your match."
