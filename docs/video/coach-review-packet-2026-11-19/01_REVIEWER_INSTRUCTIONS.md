# Independent coaching review: instructions for the reviewer

Thank you for reviewing. This packet asks for your football judgement on short match clips, and then on
some draft feedback text. There are no right answers expected, and nothing here has been scored for you.

## What you receive

- `clips/clip_01.mp4` … `clip_08.mp4`: eight unedited excerpts (18–41 s each, no sound, no overlays,
  about 4 min in total) from one amateur match filmed on a phone from the touchline.
- `02_PART_A_answer_sheet.md`: fill in first, using only the clips.
- `03_PART_B_SEALED.md`: open **only after Part A is finished and saved**. It contains draft feedback
  text written for the player, and asks whether the clips support it.
- `04_reviewer_record.md`: your background and any prior contact with this match or this tool.

The owner will show you which player is the subject (in person or by a description of shirt/kit and
starting position) before you start. That is the only information you get about the player.

## How to work

1. Fill in `04_reviewer_record.md`.
2. Watch every clip at normal speed at least once. Pause and rewind as you like.
3. Answer Part A. Cite times as `clip_NN mm:ss` (time inside the clip, from the player's controls).
4. Save Part A. Do not change it after opening Part B.
5. Open Part B and answer it. You may rewatch clips.

## Rating scale used in Part B

- **Supported**: the clips show enough to back the statement as written.
- **Overstated**: the clips show something related, but the statement claims more (scope, certainty,
  or generality) than they show.
- **Insufficient evidence**: the clips do not show enough to judge the statement either way.

Please always add a timestamp or a short reason. "Insufficient evidence" is a useful answer, not a failure.

## Please do not

- Look for other footage of the match, or ask the owner what a tool "found".
- Discuss your answers with anyone before both parts are returned.
- Share the clips. They show identifiable people and are provided only for this review.
