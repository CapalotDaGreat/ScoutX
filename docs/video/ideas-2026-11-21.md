# Ideas from open source, competing apps and games (2026-11-21)

These are leads, not results. The rules are unchanged:
- Identity uses shirt number, kit and owner confirmation only, with no face or body re-identification (LEGAL-1).
- Code, weight and dataset licences are checked separately before anything is used.
- Anything SoccerNet-derived is research-only, so it can't be used commercially.

## 1. Engine (accuracy, speed)

| # | Idea | Source | Fit for PitchProof | Licence / policy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Pre-train the number reader on synthetic jersey digits, then use real crops only for evaluation | Bhargavi et al., "Jersey number detection using synthetic data in a low-data regime" (Frontiers AI 2022 / arXiv 2203.00734): about +9 % overall and +18 % on rare numbers | Attacks the measured bottleneck: pale, small digits | Own generator, OFL fonts, Apache-2.0 docTR | **Kit ready**: `workers/football-cv/bench/e9/` (GPU session) |
| E2 | Pose keypoints to place the torso box instead of fixed bands (`ocr.BANDS` = 10–52 % of the crop height) | Koshkina & Elder CVPRW 2024 (pose-based torso + PARSeq); RTMPose (arXiv 2303.07399; 90+ FPS on an i7 CPU for RTMPose-m); `rtmlib` runs RTMPose from ONNX without mmcv (Apache-2.0) | Turned or leaning players move the number out of the fixed band | rtmlib code Apache-2.0; **RTMPose "body7" weights are trained on 7 datasets, so each dataset licence must be checked** | Next CPU experiment: torso-box hit rate on the 245 labelled target crops |
| E3 | Vote within a tracklet only after splitting tracklets on conflicting numbers; merge same-number tracklets only under time, physical and team checks | SoccerNet GSR 2025 report (arXiv 2508.19182); Golovkin et al. 2025 (arXiv 2504.06357) | Mostly present (teleport split T, conflict abstention) | Papers only | Already covered |
| E4 | Motion-agnostic association with expanded IoU boxes (no Kalman) for fast, irregular motion | Deep-EIoU (arXiv 2306.13074); GTATrack, SoccerTrack 2025 winner (arXiv 2602.00484) | Could reduce fragmentation after pans | **Its deep re-ID part is excluded (LEGAL-1)**; only expansion IoU is usable; repo licence to check | Candidate after fragmentation is measured on a 2nd labelled match |
| E5 | Vision-language model reads numbers inside a tracklet, then majority vote | SoccerNet GSR 2025 entries with LLaMA-3.2-Vision (arXiv 2508.19182) | Higher recall on hard crops | Weight licence, GPU cost, privacy (local only) | Blocked (VLM-1) |
| E6 | Static INT8 detector | This repo, `docs/video/speed-cost-2026-11-21.md` | 2.5× faster stage 1 on CPU | Same weights | Needs the IMG_4809 identity replay |

## 3. Games

| # | Pattern | Source | PitchProof translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Attributes of a player who is not fully scouted are shown as a **range**; more scouting narrows it | Football Manager manual (Sports Interactive, players page) | Show stats as ranges that narrow with more confirmed minutes, instead of single numbers or "unknown". This fits the engine's honest abstention and its uncalibrated scores |
| G2 | "Highlight key attributes" per position | Football Manager manual | Per-position focus in the match report (what matters for a full-back vs a striker) |
| G3 | Youth guidance in FM community guides: physical attributes develop first, then technical, then mental | FM youth-development guides | Product framing only (game lore, not sports science). Do **not** present it as evidence |
| G4 | Player cards with a few headline ratings, shared socially | EA SPORTS FC (general knowledge) | Share cards may show only **measured** values, with "not measured" visible. Never imitate the card design or ratings |
