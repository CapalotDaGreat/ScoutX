## 2. What the Veo angle means for PitchProof uploads (hypotheses to test, not results)

Veo uploads will usually be the **follow-cam MP4**: a 1080p virtual crop of a panorama shot from 5–7 m on the
halfway line (R7, R8). Compared with today's single test recording (handheld phone, low, near the touchline):

| Property | Expected effect | Engine stage at risk | Smallest test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Players far away and small (crop of a 180° panorama) | Fewer legible numbers; more abstention | OCR, number seeds | Downscale IMG_4809 frames 0.5× / 0.35× and rerun `bench/ocr_crop_eval.py`; measure target "20" reads per crop scale |
| Digital pan/zoom, no hand shake | Camera-motion model sees smooth global motion and zoom jumps | `cmc.py`, teleport split | Synthetic crop path over IMG_4809 with zoom steps; check teleport-split false triggers |
| Elevated view (more top-down) | Back numbers foreshortened; shorts numbers less visible | colour/shorts reader | Recorded Veo footage only (cannot be simulated honestly) |
| Stitch seam near the centre | Box splits/merges at the seam | tracker, contact guard | Recorded Veo footage only |

**Data rule:** only consented recordings from the owner's own team (downloaded by a team member, R8) can join a
held-out test set. No scraping, no SoccerNet video (R5, R6) for training or commercial evaluation.

## 3. Ideas mapped to observed failures

| Idea | Observed failure it addresses | Expected benefit | Cost / risk | Smallest falsifiable experiment | Keep / reject |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep tracklet number conflicts as abstention (R1, R3 vote inside tracklets, but only after splitting) | A seed tracklet may contain both "20" and another two-digit number; today `tw > ow` makes it a seed | Fewer unsupported seeds | Can lose a few correct seeds | Diagnostic replay logging seed conflicts (in progress, `diag_*`) | Keep only if conflicted seeds exist and removing them costs < 5 correct labelled frames with 0 new wrong |
| Count correlated reads once per run of consecutive frames | `number_votes` counts every frame; 5 reads of one blurry run look like 5 votes | Exclusion and seeding less sensitive to one bad run | Slightly fewer exclusions | Same diagnostic: count distinct runs vs reads in seeds and exclusions | Keep if any decision flips on labelled data with no new wrong |
| Legibility filter before OCR (R1) | Reads on blurred/turned crops (E2: binarised reader misses legible "20"s, colour reader adds reads) | Fewer false reads, less OCR time | Needs a trained classifier; R1 weights are SoccerNet-derived (not usable) | Heuristic first: sharpness + box height gate on existing crops; measure false two-digit reads on 1,221 team-mate crops | Keep if false reads drop and target "20" reads do not |
| Number-consistent merging only under physical checks (R3) | Fragmentation (many short target pieces) | More continuity | Risk of merging look-alikes | Already partly present (anchored continuity); no new experiment until fragmentation is measured | — |
| VLM jersey reader (R4) | Missed legible numbers | Higher recall | Weights licence, GPU cost, privacy (local only) | Blocked (VLM-1) | — |

## 4. Product patterns worth copying (no accuracy claims)

- Select the player by **number first**, then confirm on a frame (Veo player bar + circle overlay, R9).
  PitchProof already does number + "this is me" taps.
- Let the **player claim their own number** per match (R9): matches PitchProof's owner flow.
- Show **setup guidance** before recording: unique numbers, contrasting shirts, numbers not covered, camera high
  and centred (R7, R9). Cheap, and it attacks the main failure cause (illegible numbers) at the source.
- Moments as a **playlist linked to clips** (R9): matches PitchProof moments; keep the identity state visible on
  each clip (CONFIRMED vs LIKELY).

## 5. Update 2026-11-17: what limits accuracy now, and what could move it

**Sources added.**
- SoccerNet 2025 results (arXiv 2508.19182): the GSR entries split tracklets on inconsistent identity predictions,
  merge them under spatial-temporal and identity constraints, vote within tracklets, and use torso crops from pose
  keypoints before PARSeq.
- Golovkin et al. (2504.06357), the GSR 2024 winner: merges tracklets that share a jersey number only if they are
  temporally, physically and team-consistent.
- PnLCalib / No Bells Just Whistles (2404.08401, github.com/mguti97): pitch registration from keypoints and lines.
  Its models are trained on SoccerNet / WorldCup data; the weight licences must be checked before any use.
- `mkoshkina/jersey-number-pipeline`: **CC BY-NC 3.0, hockey data on request only.** Not usable for PitchProof,
  which is commercial.

**Observed bottleneck (IMG_4809, E8).**
- Without taps, the engine never acquires intervals 4 and 8 (section 14 of the ledger).
- The crops there show the back "20" clearly to a human (about 25 px digit height, back view), but neither PARSeq
  nor EasyOCR reads it, on binarised or colour regions.
- A forced check ("does a colour torso region decode to exactly 20?") fires on 15 of 245 target crops (6 of 19
  pieces), including one in interval 4 and two in interval 8. It fires on **0 of 1,221 same-kit team-mate crops**
  (maximum 0.0).
- So the limit is the recogniser on small, pale digits, not cropping, voting or thresholds.

**Ranked levers.**

| Lever | Expected benefit | Cost / risk | Smallest falsifiable experiment | Keep / reject |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jersey-specific recogniser: fine-tune docTR PARSeq (Apache-2.0) on **synthetic** jersey digits (OFL fonts, shirt colours, blur, fold, perspective) | The only lever that attacks the measured failure | GPU hours; synthetic-to-real gap | Train on synthetic data only, then test on the 245 + 1,221 real labelled crops | Keep if target "20" crops rise clearly with **0** team-mate "20" reads and no rise in other-number reads on target crops |
| Weak verification reads as LIKELY evidence (two exact-"20" colour-region reads ≥ 0.9 in one piece, ≥ 0.4 s apart, kit match, no conflicting read) | +1 piece on IMG_4809 (interval 8, about 2.6 s) | Weakens the 5.0 rule "weak-only sightings never become identity evidence"; one recording | Replay with the rule behind a flag, leave-one-out | **Not implemented:** too small a gain for relaxing an evidence rule; revisit with independent labels |
| Test-time variants of colour regions (E8b, running) | Maybe a few more target reads | More false reads | Same crop set | See ledger |
| Pitch registration (PnLCalib-style) | Distance/speed, nearest-opponent in metres | Licence of weights; handheld touchline footage is far from broadcast | Annotate 20 frames' pitch points by hand, measure the reprojection error | Not started |
| Second labelled recording, ideally a Veo follow-cam | Generalisation evidence for every candidate | Needs owner footage + consent | — | **Highest priority for promotion** |
