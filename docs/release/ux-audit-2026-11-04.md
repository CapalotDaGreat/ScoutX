## Candid product assessment

**Player problem:** Yes — a coherent Passport + match log + cautious coaching from own footage is a real gap vs club tools (Hudl/Veo) and vs encyclopedia profiles (Transfermarkt).

**Scout problem:** Partially — discovery + shortlist exist, but evidence density depends on player opt-in and logged film. Not a Wyscout replacement.

**Primary audience:** Player-first identity and development; scout as secondary consumer of opted-in Passports.

**Analysis credibility:** Appropriate only when identity confirmed and coverage sufficient; product correctly abstains. Not reliable enough to promise continuous tracking grades.

**Retention:** Strongest loop is log match → confirm identity → one concrete drill → next match. Weak if users expect automatic full-match stats without capture setup.

**Differentiation:** Player-owned evidence + honesty about limits. Weak if marketed as automatic elite analysis.

**Commercial prospects:** Plausible for a **controlled pilot** with academies/players who already film matches. Not ready to claim App Store growth or scout-market share. Acquisition will fight Hudl/Veo brand recognition; win on privacy, opt-in Passport, and truthful coaching — not feature parity.
